## What went wrong in the drill itself

The first attempt (22:43) was cut short: `aws rds wait db-instance-available` gives up after 30
minutes, and this restore plus its first backup took 33. The script then deleted the copy as
designed. Fixed in `scripts/dr/restore-drill.sh` (waits up to an hour, stops early on a failed
restore); the second attempt above used the fix.
